Why This Podcast

About & Hosts

Behind The Regs is the podcast for everyone who lives and breathes building control across the UK. Every episode pulls back the curtain on the regulations, the Gateway process, and the real judgement calls that surveyors and inspectors make on site.

We talk to the people actually doing the work — approved inspectors, fire engineers, principal designers and local authority surveyors — about what the regs really mean in practice, where the industry is heading post-Grenfell, and the stories you won't find in the guidance documents.

No jargon for jargon's sake. Just straight conversations, behind the regs.
